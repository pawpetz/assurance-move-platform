# Assurance Move Platform

Build a Complete Professional Website & Simple Booking Platform for Assurance Trucking LLC

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Build a modern, professional, mobile-first website and lightweight booking/management platform for Assurance Trucking LLC, a small owner-operated trucking and moving business.

Website: https://assurancetruckingllc.com

The goal is NOT to build a complicated enterprise logistics system.

The owner is currently operating the business largely by himself, so the platform must be extremely simple for him to manage from his phone while giving customers a polished, trustworthy, professional experience.

The philosophy should be:

Simple for the owner. Professional for the customer.

The platform should make it easy for a customer to:

	1.	Understand the business

	2.	Choose a service

	3.	Request a quote

	4.	Provide pickup and drop-off information

	5.	Upload photos

	6.	Schedule a preferred date/time

	7.	Receive a quote

	8.	Accept the quote

	9.	Pay a deposit or invoice

	10.	Receive confirmations and updates

	11.	Track the status of their job

The owner should be able to:

	1.	See booking requests

	2.	Review customer information

	3.	Review uploaded photos

	4.	Create/send a quote

	5.	Accept or decline a booking

	6.	Schedule the job

	7.	Collect payments

	8.	Update job status

	9.	Contact the customer

	10.	View upcoming jobs in a simple calendar

	11.	Manage customers

	12.	Manage basic business settings

Do NOT build unnecessary driver management, fleet management, employee accounts, complex dispatching, or complicated GPS systems at this stage.
